01 / PROFILE

About

My research interests lie at the intersection of geophysical inversion, seismic methods, and computational tools for monitoring the subsurface. I am particularly interested in methods that make geophysical interpretation more robust under imperfect data conditions and useful for time-lapse applications.

I value a balance between mathematical formulation, numerical implementation, and geophysical interpretation—connecting algorithmic development with problems in energy and environmental monitoring.

2D Magnetotelluric Inversion with Optimal Transport

Developing an open-source PyTorch workflow using optimal-transport-based data misfit and graph-space formulations, with an emphasis on inversion behaviour under static-shift and data-distribution differences.
